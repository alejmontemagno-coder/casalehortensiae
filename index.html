The Farmhouse

A home where
hospitality is a
human gesture

“An ecosystem generates no waste, since the waste of one species is the food of another.”— Fritjof Capra

Casale Hortensiae is a former farmhouse shaped by cosmopolitan taste, nestled in the green Tiber Valley, just minutes from the Orte exit on the A1 motorway. A place that feels far from everything — yet Rome is half an hour by train, Viterbo and its thermal spas are nearby, and Umbria is just around the corner.

A few hundred metres of slightly bumpy road — the first invitation to slow down — will be rewarded with family warmth, a fireplace in winter, a garden with pool in summer, real wool mattresses to rest on, and a breakfast that is beautiful, good and organic.

People from all over the world meet here, many languages are spoken, and everyone gathers around the table — by the fire in winter, outdoors in summer.

Cuisine Sauvage

The kitchen that
never repeats itself

The concept of Cuisine Sauvage comes from culinary improvisation — cooking with whatever you find around you at that moment.

We have created a synergic vegetable garden from which we gather fresh produce day by day: hummus, herb and flower salads, soups, grains, fruit tarts. Impossible to give recipes — not because they are secret, but because everything is created on impulse with ingredients that are never the same twice.

Our bread is made with freshly milled living flour, left to rise for three days with our lovingly tended sourdough starter. The salad is a mix of herbs and flowers, freshly picked fruit and seeds.

“Even the soup is always different. There is always something bubbling on the stove here.”

Our philosophy

We are the New Rurals

We are fully aligned with those who have chosen to return to the land, to live slowly at a natural pace, to cultivate a sustainable future.

It is not a trend, not a label. It is a way of being in the world that we practice every day — through the vegetable garden, solar panels, sourdough, medicinal herbs gathered from the woods.

“We have chosen to live slowly at a natural pace. We are the ones who still feel moved by a sunset, who rejoice at a flower blooming.”

“When the wind of change blows, some build walls. We build windmills.”

— From the New Rurals Manifesto

Sustainability

Small gestures,
every day

Not a certification. A way of living we have practiced long before it became fashionable: solar water heating, photovoltaics, zero waste, organic garden, WiFi off at night.

For electro-sensitive guests, we can switch everything off and be completely wave-free.

Solar energy

We heat water with the sun. Photovoltaic system to reduce grid consumption.

Mindful WiFi

We turn off WiFi when not needed. For electro-sensitive guests, zero waves on request.

Synergic garden

Seasonal vegetables grown by us. Wild herbs, biodiversity, permaculture.

Zero waste

We reuse all our scraps. Producing as little waste as possible is a daily practice.

Precious water

Careful consumption, rainwater harvesting for the garden.

Short supply chain

Local ingredients, nearby producers, respected seasonality.
